# cards — requirements

- **REQ-CARD-001** The card index page lists all cards in a grid, each showing title and
  cover image.
- **REQ-CARD-002** The card detail page shows the card description and a **Download action
  row** (per design, `figma:2:2`) with the download button and file-size hint.
- **REQ-CARD-003** The detail page toolbar offers **Export as PDF** (`figma:2:3`).
- **REQ-CARD-004** A **Share-by-email popup** opens from the detail page (`figma:2:4`),
  with recipient field and send button.
- **REQ-CARD-005** Card grid items show the designed **hover style** (elevation shadow on
  hover).
- **REQ-CARD-006** After a download completes, the member receives an **email receipt**.
